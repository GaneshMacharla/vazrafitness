Our method

Consistency beats motivation

మా పద్ధతి

Most people don't quit because training is hard. They quit because nobody is watching. At VAJRA, someone always is.

Step 1

Free trial and assessment

Train with a coach, check your body composition and talk through your goal.

Step 2

A plan written for you

Your training split and a simple food guide, built around your work hours.

Step 3

Form first, weight later

Your coach teaches every main lift before you start adding plates.

Step 4

Check-ins that keep you honest

Missed three days? Expect a WhatsApp message from your coach.

Step 5

Measure, adjust, repeat

A fresh scan every four weeks, so your plan changes as your body does.
